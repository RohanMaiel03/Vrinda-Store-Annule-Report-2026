# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2026

## Project Overview

The **Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2026** is an Excel-based data analysis project focused on analyzing the store's sales performance and customer purchasing behavior throughout the year 2026.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights using Microsoft Excel, including data cleaning, data analysis, Pivot Tables, charts, and dashboards.

## Objectives

- Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior based on gender.
- Identify the top-performing states by sales.
- Analyze order status distribution.
- Understand the relationship between customer age and gender.
- Identify the highest-performing sales channels.
- Determine the best-selling product categories.

## Business Questions & Analysis

This project answers the following business questions:

1. **Sales vs Orders:** Compare sales and the number of orders using a single chart.
2. **Monthly Performance:** Which month recorded the highest sales and orders?
3. **Gender Analysis:** Who purchased more in 2026 – men or women?
4. **Order Status:** What were the different order statuses in 2026?
5. **State-wise Sales:** Which top 10 states contributed the most to sales?
6. **Age & Gender Analysis:** What is the relationship between customer age and gender based on the number of orders?
7. **Sales Channel Analysis:** Which sales channel contributed the maximum sales?
8. **Category Analysis:** Which product category had the highest sales?

1. **Data Understanding:** Reviewed the raw Vrinda Store sales dataset.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked and corrected inconsistencies, duplicate records, and formatting issues.

5. **Dashboard Creation:** Designed an interactive dashboard to present important business insights in a clear and understandable format.
